## Review summary

The idempotency core (BEGIN IMMEDIATE reservation, claim-token-guarded writes) holds. The defects
sat on the seams: the uvicorn factory contract, stdlib `sqlite3` transaction semantics, the typed
SQLite round-trip into strict pydantic models, the process-wide logging/traceback path, and
provenance of mock output kept across restarts.
